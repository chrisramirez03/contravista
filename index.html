Master Formula Reference (EE 101)

Ohm's Law
$$V = I \cdot R$$

Voltage equals current times resistance

Units: Volts (V), Amperes (A), Ohms (Ω)
Current & Resistance
$$I = \frac{V}{R} \quad\text{and}\quad R = \frac{V}{I}$$

Current flow & opposition relations

Units: Amperes (A) & Ohms (Ω)
Electrical Power (Watt's Law)
$$P = V \cdot I = I^2 R = \frac{V^2}{R}$$

Rate of electrical energy conversion into work/heat

Units: Watts (W) = Joules per second (J/s)
Kirchhoff's Laws (KCL & KVL)
$$\sum I_{\text{in}} = \sum I_{\text{out}} \quad\text{and}\quad \sum V_{\text{loop}} = 0$$

Conservation of charge at nodes & energy around closed loops

Foundation for Nodal & Mesh Analysis
Voltage Divider Rule
$$V_{\text{out}} = V_{\text{in}} \cdot \left(\frac{R_2}{R_1 + R_2}\right)$$

Fractional voltage drop across series resistors

Valid for unloaded series resistor pairs
Equivalent Resistances
$$R_{\text{series}} = \sum R_i \quad\text{and}\quad R_{\text{parallel}} = \frac{R_1 \cdot R_2}{R_1 + R_2}$$

Series resistors add; parallel resistors combine reciprocally

Units: Ohms (Ω)
Maximum Power Transfer
$$R_L = R_{\text{Th}} \implies P_{\text{max}} = \frac{V_{\text{Th}}^2}{4 R_{\text{Th}}}$$

Matched load impedance maximizes power delivery (50% efficiency)

Thevenin Source Matching
Capacitors & Inductors
$$i_C = C \frac{dv_C}{dt} \quad\text{and}\quad v_L = L \frac{di_L}{dt}$$

Energy: \(w_C = \frac{1}{2} C V^2\)  |  \(w_L = \frac{1}{2} L I^2\)

Capacitor = Open in DC  |  Inductor = Short in DC
First-Order Time Constants (τ)
$$\tau = R \cdot C \quad\text{and}\quad v(t) = V_s \left(1 - e^{-t/\tau}\right)$$

Step charging transient • Settles in 5τ (99.3% steady state)

Units: Seconds (s)
AC Complex Impedance
$$\mathbf{Z}_R = R, \quad \mathbf{Z}_L = j\omega L, \quad \mathbf{Z}_C = \frac{1}{j\omega C}$$

Ohm's Law in frequency domain: \(\mathbf{V} = \mathbf{I} \cdot \mathbf{Z}\)

Resonance: \(\omega_0 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{LC}}\)  |  \(f_c = \frac{1}{2\pi RC}\)
Op-Amp Golden Rules & Gain
$$A_{v\text{(inv)}} = -\frac{R_f}{R_1} \quad\text{and}\quad A_{v\text{(non-inv)}} = 1 + \frac{R_f}{R_1}$$

Ideal Op-Amp parameters under negative feedback (\(i_+ = i_- = 0, v_+ = v_-\))

Buffer: \(A_v = 1\) (Unity Gain Voltage Follower)
Decoupling & Switching
$$C_{\text{bypass}} \ge 0.1\,\mu\text{F} \quad\text{and}\quad I_c = \beta \cdot I_b$$

Place within 3mm of IC Vcc pin & solid unbroken ground plane
